the tape deck

a bench rig for docs/riffs/tape-deck.md. two hands, one tape, two packet shapes. the tape never runs backward; the head does.

the tape between them

each hand is its own copy of the spool; the plug is the rig's whole transport. plugged, every packet crosses at once. unplugged, each hand keeps its own worldview until you plug them back in. the first two packets are the rig's, so the tape isn't blank. memory-only — a reload starts over, and nothing here touches a relay.

the script: wind a color on one hand and watch it land on the other. unplug. on each hand, wind +1 and then a color. plug back in — same packets, same order, both sides. step a head back once: color disagrees, count agrees. undo mine, show ghosts, then rewind and put it back — the walk leaves footprints.
